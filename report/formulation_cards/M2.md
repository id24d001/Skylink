# M2 — Hub plate topology: formulation card

_Draft written with AI help from the code in `src/m2_plate/`. **Rewrite in your own words before hand-in** (rule 1)._

**Question.** How light can the 160 × 160 × 3 mm 6061-T6 centre plate be while staying within 2.5× the compliance of a solid plate, and what outline do we cut?

**Model.** In hover the arms push the plate up at the corners and the battery pushes down in the middle, so the plate works in **out-of-plane bending**: a Kirchhoff plate (ACM element, w and two slopes per node), not plane stress. Quarter model with symmetry on both mid-lines (zero slope across the cut, filter mirrored), checked against the whole plate.

**Decision variables.** Element densities x_e ∈ [0, 1], continuous: a 40 × 40 quarter mesh (2 mm, default) or 80 × 80 (1 mm, `SKYLINK_M2_MESH=fine`). The volume fraction vf is swept as a parameter, 0.20–0.50.

**Objective and constraint.** Minimise compliance c = fᵀu subject to K(x̃)u = f and mean(x̃) = vf. SIMP: E(x̃) = E_min + x̃³(E₀ − E_min), E_min = 1e-9. x̃ = Hx is the **density filter** (linear hat, r = 6 mm), as the brief asks, not a sensitivity filter. Optimality-criteria update (move 0.2, stop when the largest change < 0.01).

**From grey field to a cuttable part.** (1) Threshold at the level that keeps vf. (2) Open with a 6 mm disk; members thinner than 6 mm are **widened** by 3 mm, not deleted (deleting cut the load path at low vf). (3) Close with the same disk to fill slots under 6 mm, then open again so convex corners get R 3 mm. (4) This passes the 6 mm test by construction (every solid point lies in a solid 6 mm disk); the narrowest member is also measured. (5) The cut part is re-analysed on a 1 mm mesh; its outline goes to `results/m2_plate_outline.dxf`.

**Selection.** The lightest cut part with compliance ≤ **2.4×** solid, swept in 0.05 steps, then 0.01 steps near the limit.

**Result.** vf = 0.32, giving a cut part of vf 0.332 and **108.7 g** (brief formula: 0.332 × 207.4 g + 40 g). Compliance 2.31× solid (2.33× when designed on a 1.33 mm mesh). Narrowest member 6.75 mm. Under 186 N, w_max = 0.73 mm (solid 0.32 mm) and peak bending stress 107 MPa against 276 MPa yield (factor 2.6, already at 2 g). The shape is an X: four tapered arms join a ~65 mm central pad.

**Back into M1 (re-run once).** Plate 0.25 → 0.109 kg (−141 g). Continuous optima fall by 229–536 g (1.6–3.8× the plate saving, because mass snowballs); snapped designs by 141–447 g. Parts change for 4 of 5 designs; all stay feasible, on both the FM0 and surrogate-FM paths (`results/m2_m1_feedback.csv`).

**Verification** (appendix): MBB benchmark (density filter, no filter → checkerboards, p = 1 → grey); ACM element vs Navier solution, 0.03 % error at 64²; quarter = whole plate; adjoint gradients vs finite differences, 1e-6; mesh check.

**Key assumptions and data issues handled.**
- *Material:* 6061-T6 handbook values E = 68.9 GPa, ν = 0.33, yield 276 MPa, 2,700 kg/m³ (the brief names only the alloy and density); linear elastic, small deflection, thin plate (t/L ≈ 0.02).
- *Supports:* w = 0 on a 10 × 10 mm arm-clamp patch at each corner, rotations free (simply supported, conservative: a tube clamp gives a 3 mm plate little rotational restraint); the patches are kept solid. Any 8–15 mm patch is defensible (user to confirm 10 mm).
- *Load:* one load case, the battery only, as a uniform downward pressure on the central 40 × 40 mm. F = 2 g × 9.48 kg = 186 N: 2 g from T_max = 2mg/4 (steepest climb), 9.48 kg the heaviest pack set in the M1 design set (E). Payload, pod and avionics are not carried through the plate. The load size scales deflection and stress only, not the topology or any compliance ratio.
- *Not modelled:* in-plane loads (drag, yaw torque), carried by membrane action, far stiffer than bending at t = 3 mm; bolt holes (clamps sit on the solid corner patches, the battery strap goes around the central pad).
- *SIMP settings:* p = 3, filter radius 6 mm = the minimum member width (physical, so mesh independent).
- *2.4× target, not 2.5×:* a 4 % margin because the ratio moves about 1 % with the mesh; costs 2.4 g (user may prefer exactly 2.5×, 106.3 g).
- *Mass:* the brief's formula with the cut part's own area fraction (0.332), not the nominal vf, plus 40 g fasteners.
