# M0 — Problem framing: formulation card

**Question.** Which drone system (vehicle, hubs, fleet, policy) should the DHO buy so every PHC gets emergency items fast, at the lowest defensible annual cost?

**Stakeholders and what each wants:**

| Stakeholder | Wants | Plan |
|----------------|----------------------------------------------|----------------|
| DHO | every PHC covered, few late emergencies, a cost to defend | O1, O2, C1, C3 |
| PHC staff | blood/antivenom < 45 min, routine same day, labs collected | O2, O3, C2, C4 |
| Blood bank (H0) | cold chain kept, nothing lost or stuck in a queue | C2, C5, C6 |
| Finance | lowest yearly spend, no open-ended cost | O1, C7 |

**Objectives (three, all used again in M6):**

- O1 min annual cost, ₹/yr (capex × CRF at 10 % + opex + staff + energy + road trips)
- O2 max S45, share of emergencies delivered within 45 min of the order, any mode
- O3 min road-fallback share. P95 emergency time is reported only.

**Everything else as a constraint with a number:**

| # | Constraint | Number |
|----|--------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| C1 | PHCs reachable by drone | 24 of 24 (radius ≥ 18.4 km; P17 binds) |
| C2 | farthest PHC trip, no queue | ≤ 45 min (P17: 3 + 20.3 + 1 = 24.3 min) |
| C3 | reliability floor | mean S45 ≥ 90 % |
| C4 | routine and lab orders | delivered same day; left at 24:00 → road (₹500) |
| C5 | payload incl. insulated box | ≤ 2.5 kg |
| C6 | emergency queue wait | ≤ 30 min, then road (₹2,500, 120 min) |
| C7 | annual cost | ≤ ₹5.57 crore/yr |
| C8 | drone utilisation per hub | ≤ 70 % (M4) |
| C9 | flying conditions | no take-off at gust ≥ 14 m/s; avoid 2 no-fly zones; 15 % reserve |
| C10 | outage power (M/W/F 14–17 h) | ≤ 3 kW per hub, so ≤ 2 bays charging |
| C11 | staff | 1 per 4 drones per shift, ≥ 1 per hub; 3 shifts × 1.2 |

**Decision hierarchy and variable types:**

(refer to figures/m0_decision_hierarchy.png for design hierarchy)

1. *Vehicle (M1–M3):* D, E, d, t, V and the M2 plate densities **continuous**; battery type, motor, tube **categorical**; pack count, prop inches **integer**. Out: designs A–E, radius 10–24 km.
2. *Hub network (M4):* y_j (8 sites), x_ij (24 × 8) **binary**; drones n_j, bays b_j **integer**; design **categorical** (one MILP each).
3. *Fleet and policy (M5–M6):* design A–E **categorical**; drones, bays, spare packs per hub **integer**; emergency reserve fraction **continuous** in [0, 1].
4. *Recommendation (M7):* one system and the decision rule, **categorical**.

**Key assumptions and data issues handled:**

- "reliable" = mean S45 ≥ 90 % (C3, from the M7 test)
- "controlled cost" = ≤ ₹5.57 crore/yr, the all-road cost today (27.36 × ₹2,500 + 168.3 × ₹500, × 365) (C7)
- "every PHC" = 24/24 reachable by drone, a hard constraint (C1)
- *"45 min" split in two:* C2 is the no-queue trip in still air at 15 m/s (framing value; each design's own V is used in M4); waiting time counts only in O2
- *Demand:* Poisson at the demand.csv means (27.36 emergency, 94.77 routine, 73.53 lab /day), the same every day (no weekday, seasonal or growth trend until M7's +15 %/yr test); only PHCs generate orders; operations 24 h × 365 d
- *Wind:* the one-year hourly file is a typical year at every site
- *Cost:* all capex annualised with CRF at 10 % over the costs.yaml lives; no inflation, salvage value or land cost beyond the hub capex; road is always available at the costs.yaml price and time
- *Data issue 1 (rule 5):* hourly_profile.csv routine column sums to **1.1993** (others ≈ 1). demand.csv is taken as the true daily total, so each column is rescaled to sum to 1 in code (`m0_framing.clean_hourly_profile`) for M4 and M5; the raw file is not edited
- *Data issue 2:* components.csv has 28 tubes, not 30 (CF35×0.75, CF40×0.75 missing); M1 snaps to those 28
- The other 16 checks pass (results/m0_data_checks.csv).
