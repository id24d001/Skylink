# M0 — Problem framing: formulation card

_Draft written with AI help. **Rewrite in your own words before hand-in** (rule 1). Numbers come from `results/m0_framing_numbers.csv` and `results/m0_data_checks.csv`, made by `python run_all.py --only m0`._

**Question.** Which drone system (vehicle, hubs, fleet, policy) should the DHO buy so that every PHC gets emergency items fast, at the lowest annual cost that can be defended?

**Stakeholders and what each wants.**

| Stakeholder | Wants | Becomes |
|---|---|---|
| DHO | every PHC covered, few late emergencies, a cost to defend | O1, O2, C1, C3 |
| PHC staff | blood/antivenom in < 45 min, routine stock same day, lab samples collected | O2, O3, C2, C4 |
| Blood bank (H0) | cold chain kept, nothing lost or stuck in a queue | C2, C5, C6 |
| Finance | lowest yearly spend, no open-ended cost | O1, C7 |

**Objectives (three, all used again in M6).** O1 min annual cost, ₹/yr (capex × CRF at 10 % + opex + staff + energy + road trips). O2 max S45, the share of emergency orders delivered within 45 min of the order by any mode. O3 min road-fallback share. The 95th-percentile emergency time is reported, not optimised.

**Every other wish as a constraint with a number.**

| # | Constraint | Number | Why a constraint, not an objective |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | PHCs reachable by drone | 24 of 24 (needs radius ≥ 18.4 km, P17 is the bottleneck) | "every PHC" is a promise, not a trade |
| C2 | farthest PHC, no queue: load + flight + drop | ≤ 45 min (P17, 18.3 km at 15 m/s: 3 + 20.3 + 1 = 24.3 min) | the hard part of "45 min"; waiting is in O2 |
| C3 | reliability floor for a candidate system | mean S45 ≥ 90 % (M7 tests P(S45 ≥ 90 %)) | gives "reliable" a number; below it a system is rejected |
| C4 | routine and lab orders | delivered same day; left at 24:00 → road (₹500) | no value in beating "same day" |
| C5 | payload incl. insulated box | ≤ 2.5 kg | fixed by the box |
| C6 | emergency queue wait | ≤ 30 min, then road (₹2,500, 120 min) | given operating rule |
| C7 | annual cost | ≤ ₹5.57 crore/yr (all orders by road today) | ceiling; anything dearer is not worth buying |
| C8 | drone utilisation per hub | ≤ 70 % (M4) | keeps slack for Poisson peaks |
| C9 | flying conditions | no take-off at gust ≥ 14 m/s; no entry into 2 no-fly zones; 15 % energy reserve | safety, not negotiable |
| C10 | power in grid outage (Mon/Wed/Fri 14–17 h) | ≤ 3 kW per hub, so ≤ 2 bays charging | genset size is given |
| C11 | staff | 1 operator per 4 drones per shift, ≥ 1 per open hub, 3 shifts × 1.2 | labour rule; costs ₹15.1 lakh/yr per hub minimum |

Vehicle limits (stress ≤ 200 MPa, deflection ≤ 0.01 L, f1 margin 1.15, T ≤ 100 N) are on the M1 card.

**Decision hierarchy and variable types** (figure `figures/m0_decision_hierarchy.png`). Each level fixes the inputs of the next; costs from M4 and the M6 front feed back to choose the vehicle.
1. *Vehicle (M1–M3):* D, E, d, t, V and the M2 plate densities are **continuous**; battery type, motor and tube are **categorical**; pack count and prop size in inches are **integer**. Output: designs A–E, radius 10–24 km.
2. *Hub network (M4):* open hub y_j (8 sites) and assignment x_ij (24 × 8) are **binary**; drones n_j and bays b_j are **integer**; the design used is **categorical** (one MILP per design).
3. *Fleet and policy (M5–M6):* vehicle design from A–E **categorical**; drones, bays, spare batteries per hub **integer**; emergency reserve fraction **continuous** in [0, 1].
4. *Recommendation (M7):* one system from the front and the decision rule, both **categorical**.
