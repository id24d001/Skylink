# M3 — Rotor surrogate from scarce data: formulation card

_At most one page. **AI-drafted — rewrite in your own words before submission.** Status: 🟡 draft._

**Question this module answers:** what is the rotor figure of merit FM as a function of diameter D and thrust per rotor T, built from at most 30 expensive oracle runs, so M1 can stop assuming FM0 = 0.65?

**Decision variables:**
- Where to sample: 30 (D, T) points in 3 requests (15 + 9 + 6), continuous, with D 0.35–0.80 m and T 8–40 N.
- Which surrogate to use: categorical (quadratic, RBF thin-plate, RBF cubic, GP with ARD + nugget).
- Inputs: scaled (D, T). Disk loading DL = T/(πD²/4) is used only to interpret the results.

**Objective(s):**
- Lowest prediction error at points the model has not seen. RMSE in FM units is the headline metric; Q² is secondary.
- Most accuracy where M1 needs FM, at the loaded and empty hover thrust of designs A–E.

**Constraints:**
- At most 30 runs and at most 3 requests, all inside the oracle box.
- Each new point at least 0.10 (scaled) from existing points, except deliberate repeats.
- M1 may evaluate FM only inside the convex hull of the sampled points.

**Inputs / outputs:**
- In: oracle results; M1 designs (hover thrusts) to steer requests 2 and 3; M2 plate mass for the final M1 re-run.
- Out: `ctx["fm_surrogate"]` (cubic RBF) for M1 and later modules, and the re-run M1 designs.

**Method and why:**
- Request 1: maximin LHS, a space-filling start.
- Requests 2 and 3: scored greedy selection. The split was 3/3/2/1 for request 2 and 2/3/1 for request 3 (exploitation / exploration / random, plus a repeat in request 2).
  - Exploitation score: closeness to the M1 hover points × GP σ.
  - Exploration score: 0.7·σ + 0.3·|RBF − GP|.
- Models compared by LOO, repeated 5-fold CV and true hold-out on each later request, with predictions frozen before sending.
- Cubic RBF kept: tied with the others within the CV spread, and the M1 designs do not depend on the choice.

**Verification:** final LOO / 5-fold / hold-out error table and parity plot (`results/m3_final_error_table.csv`, `figures/m3_loo_parity_final.png`), plus the noise note in the verification appendix. Local check where M1 operates (DL 40–110): cubic RBF LOO RMSE 0.017 and hold-out RMSE 0.007 at the two request-3 points next to designs C and D (`results/m3_local_error_M1_region.csv`).

**Key assumptions and data issues handled:**
- Noise is about 0.003 from one repeat, so the oracle is treated as deterministic.
- Repeated runs are averaged before fitting.
- The FM cliff above DL ≈ 180 is not trusted and is kept out of M1 by the hull constraint.
- The request-1 LHS notebook prints points that differ from `result_1.csv` (logged).

**Result:** with the real FM, M1 take-off mass rises by 4–8 % and rotors shrink, for example design C goes from 0.78 m to 0.68 m. Request 3 moved the optimum by less than 1 %.
