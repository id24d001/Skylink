# M7 — Decision and defense: formulation card

**Question.** Which of M6's finalists should the DHO buy, and does that choice survive bad weather, busy months, broken drones, better batteries, cheaper drones and growing demand?

**Decision variable:** one **categorical** choice among M6's top 3 on 365 days, all design E (hubs H1, H2, H4), i.e. the picks of the brief's three rules. Under demand growth, also the fleet re-tune (drones, spares, bays per hub, **integer**).

| Candidate | Drones | Spares | Bays | Reserve | ₹ crore/yr | S45 |
|--------------|---------|---------|---------|-----------|-----------|--------|
| **knee** | 4-3-4 | 4-4-6 | 3-2-4 | 1 per hub | 2.25 | 0.889 |
| best S45 | 5-4-4 | 4-6-5 | 3-2-2 | 1 per hub | 2.45 | 0.889 |
| no reserve | 4-3-4 | 5-5-4 | 4-2-3 | 0 | 2.20 | 0.880 |

**Measures:** annual cost (M6 cost model), S45, P(S45 ≥ 0.90), worst-day S45, road share and P95.

**Decision rule:** the **knee**, the point after which more money stops buying S45, tested by **minimax regret**: regret = cost regret + λ × S45 regret over every scenario, at three prices λ of one S45 point. The DHO set no budget below the ₹5.57 crore cap and 0.90 cannot be reached, so "best service within budget" would pay +₹0.2 crore for +0.03 pp.

**Constraints:**

| # | Constraint | Number |
|----|--------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| C1 | annual cost (M0 C7) | ≤ ₹5.57 crore/yr |
| C2 | operating rules | every M5 rule unchanged (costs.yaml) |
| C3 | demand-growth top-up | S45 within 1 pp of year 0 (73 CRN days) |

**Method:**

- *Robustness:* calm, typical and worst-monsoon wind weeks found in wind_days.csv (20 demand seeds per week day; typical = M6's 365 days and seeds)
- *Reliability:* Monte Carlo, N = 500 samples × 30 random days, same draws for all candidates (CRN), Wilson 95 % CI
- *Sensitivity:* M1 re-run at 180 Wh/kg; re-cost of M6's 3,795-configuration archive and knee re-pick at −30 % drone price; demand years 0–5 (×1.15/yr) with the fleet fixed, then a greedy re-tune

**Result:**

- S45 of the knee: 0.97 (calm week), 0.889 (year), 0.53 (worst monsoon week; worst day 0.03, grounding hits all three alike)
- P(30-day S45 ≥ 0.90) = 0.29 [0.25, 0.33] knee, 0.30 best S45, 0.16 no reserve; demand ±25 % and availability barely matter, the wind on the days drawn decides
- **battery +20 % on the same drone lifts S45 to 0.944 (target met) and cuts cost by ₹0.11 crore**; the lighter re-sized drone flies slower (17 vs 20 m/s) and loses 1.9 pp
- drone price −30 % saves ₹0.16 crore; the knee moves to 12 drones, 20 spares
- demand growth: S45 holds, cost grows; re-tuning keeps road share near 10 % for ₹2.5–4.2 crore/yr (years 1–5)
- the knee is the minimax-regret choice for λ ≥ ₹0.053 crore/pp (≈ ₹5,300 per late emergency); with the fleet frozen for 5 years, no reserve wins. **Decision: the knee**

**Verification:** V1 typical year = M6 365-day numbers exactly; V2 cost model = M6 term by term, M1 re-size at 150 Wh/kg = design E, drone-price hand check, archive re-pick at nominal = M6 knee; V3 Monte Carlo at demand 1, availability 1 = 365-day S45 within CI; V4 Wilson half-width ≤ 5 pp; V5 predicted vs actual (P1–P8, written before the first run).

**Key assumptions and data issues handled:**

- *Candidates:* M6's top 3 on 365 days; all are design E, so the wind scenarios test the policy, not the network
- *Wind scenarios:* "calm" = the 7 consecutive days with the lowest mean wind (days 40–46); "worst monsoon week" = the 7-day window ending June–September with the most gust-grounded hours (days 209–215, 67/168 h); "typical" = all 365 days; worst case = the worst day's S45, averaged over its demand seeds
- *Monte Carlo:* demand ~ U(0.75, 1.25) on every PHC and class, availability ~ U(0.90, 0.98) (the brief gives ranges only); each drone is out for a whole day with probability 1 − a, its pack not used as a spare, reserve = min(k, drones available); "S45 ≥ 0.90" is the 30-day (one month) mean, with the per-day probability also reported
- *Battery +20 %:* catalogue packs keep Wh and price per Wh, mass ÷ 1.2; (A) same drone with 20 % more energy, (B) M1 re-sized for the same 24 km still-air radius; hubs and fleet unchanged
- *Drone price −30 %:* applies to drone capex and so to the 15 % maintenance; batteries, bays and hubs unchanged
- *Demand +15 %/yr:* hubs fixed; the top-up re-tunes on cost, because S45 stays flat while road trips grow; regret uses years 1–2 only, as drones last 3 years and are re-bought then
- *Regret:* cost regret only from whole-year scenarios; λ set three ways: implied by the knee choice (₹0.055 crore/pp), road-trip value of 1 pp (₹0.025 crore), high (₹0.20 crore)
- *Out of scope:* an extra hub near P11/P17 and a headwind range margin in M1, named in the memo as the route to 0.90
