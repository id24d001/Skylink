# M5 — Discrete-event simulator: formulation card

**Question.** M4 sized the network on daily averages. When orders arrive at random, peak at 10–15 h, wait in queues, and meet real hourly wind, gust groundings and the grid outage, what service level does the M4 network actually give?

**Decision variables:** none are optimised here; M5 is the evaluator and M6 optimises its inputs. Per configuration:

- vehicle design C, D or E, **categorical** (from M1); open hubs and PHC → hub assignment fixed (from M4)
- drones n_j, bays b_j and spare packs s_j per hub, **integer** ≥ 0
- emergency reserve fraction r ∈ [0, 1], **continuous**; k_j = round-half-up(r·n_j) drones held back

**Outputs (the M6 objectives come from these):**

- S45 = share of emergencies delivered within 45 min of the order, any mode (mean of daily values, with 95 % CI)
- P95 emergency delivery time; road-fallback share (all orders, and emergencies only)
- drone utilisation (dispatch → ready again ÷ 24 h); energy in kWh/day (grid and genset)

**Rules (from the brief and costs.yaml):**

| # | Rule | Number |
|----|----------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| R1 | orders | Poisson per PHC, class and hour |
| R2 | ground times | load and take-off 3 min, drop or pick-up 1 min, swap and turnaround 6 min, 60 s hover at each end |
| R3 | flight time and energy | 2LV/(V² − w²), w = that hour's mean wind |
| R4 | gust grounding | no take-off in an hour with gust ≥ 14 m/s |
| R5 | emergency road fallback | still waiting after 30 min → road (+120 min) |
| R6 | charging | bay 1.5 kW at 90 %; outage Mon/Wed/Fri 14–17 h, 3 kW genset runs ≤ 2 bays |
| R7 | dispatch | emergencies first; the reserve is held back for them |

**Method:** each run is one 24 h day; days are drawn from the wind year and repeated until every configuration's S45 95 % CI is < ±3 pp (30-day pilot, then n = ⌈(t₀.₉₇₅ s / 0.03)²⌉), which gave **73 days**. Day k uses demand seed 2026 + k, so every configuration sees the same days and orders (common random numbers, reused in M6).

**Result (design D as M4 sized it):**

- **S45 = 0.851 ± 0.025** (target 0.90); P95 150 min, because > 5 % of emergencies go by road
- road share 45 % (emergencies 14.5 %); utilisation only 0.46. The limits are packs and bays, the reserve and wind, not drone time
- one spare pack per drone lifts S45 to 0.882
- an unlimited fleet over all 365 days reaches only **0.88 (C), 0.88 (D), 0.89 (E)**: 5–6 % of emergencies fall in gust groundings and 5–7 % are out of range in headwind (P11 and P17 from H2 sit within 0.1–1.5 km of D's still-air radius). Fleet alone cannot reach 0.90

**Verification:** one hub, exponential drone time, no wind or outage → mean wait vs Erlang C for 8 (c, ρ) cases; CI rule above; a hand-timed sortie; reserve, grounding, outage-bay, conservation and reproducibility checks.

**Key assumptions and data issues handled:**

- *Day:* starts at 00:00 with empty queues, idle drones and full packs; no warm-up, nothing carries over
- *Wind days:* drawn uniformly with replacement from the 365 days of wind_days.csv, 24 hourly rows each; day 1 is a **Monday** (sets the outage days; the file gives no weekday)
- *Orders:* rate = daily mean × hourly fraction (M0-rescaled profile), uniform times within the hour; one order = one sortie, no batching (as in M4)
- *Queues:* per hub, FIFO, non-pre-emptive; emergencies first, then routine and lab together; routine or lab may not take one of the last k_j idle drones; an order that cannot fly now (grounded, or out of range in this hour's wind) is skipped and the next one tried
- *Grounding:* loading starts only if the current hour and the take-off hour are flyable; drones in the air finish; dispatch re-tried every hour
- *Headwind:* the hour's mean wind over the whole sortie, direction ignored; a sortie flies only if its energy fits within E_use (15 % reserve kept)
- *Delivery time:* order → dispatch + 3 + leg + 1 hover + 1 drop; a lab sample counts as delivered on landing back at the hub
- *Routine and lab:* no 30 min limit; those still waiting at **24:00 go by road** ("same day"), with no KPI for their road time
- *Batteries:* one pack per drone, default **spares = 0** (as M4 costed); a landed pack queues FIFO for a bay and is recharged by the energy used (Wh/0.9 at 1.5 kW); the drone takes any charged pack, then a 6 min swap; no degradation within a day
- *Outage:* 14–17 h, bays above 2 are paused and resume after 17:00; energy drawn then is genset kWh
- *P09:* a PHC that is its own hub is served by hand-over at 0 min, no sortie
- *Out of scope:* drone breakdowns and availability < 100 % (M7), demand growth (M7), staff limits on simultaneous dispatches
