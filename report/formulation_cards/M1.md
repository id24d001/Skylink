# M1 — Vehicle MDO: formulation card

**Question.** What is the lightest quadcopter that carries 2.5 kg to a required radius, and how does its mass change as the radius the hub network needs changes?

**Decision variables:**

- **Continuous:** rotor diameter D 0.35–0.80 m, battery energy E 200–2000 Wh, arm tube outer diameter d 15–40 mm, wall t 0.5–2.5 mm, cruise speed V 8–22 m/s
- **After snapping to the catalogue:** battery pack, motor and tube **categorical**; pack count (1–4) and prop size (14–31 in) **integer**

**Objective:**

- min take-off mass m (kg) for a given required radius R_req
- max radius was rejected: its optimum sits on the D, E and d upper bounds (20.1 kg, 27.3 km), its loaded hover thrust (49 N) is outside the M3 oracle range, and drone capex grows by ₹45,000 per kg
- min mass gives one design per R_req, the family that M4 and M6 choose from

**Constraints (g ≥ 0, normalised):**

| # | Constraint | Number |
|----|--------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| C1 | radius, still air (eq. 5) | R ≥ R_req |
| C2 | arm bending stress / tip deflection | ≤ 200 MPa / ≤ 0.01 L |
| C3 | arm first frequency | f1 ≥ 1.15 × V_tip/(πD) |
| C4 | tube slenderness | d/t ≤ 40 |
| C5 | max thrust per motor | T_max = 2mg/4 ≤ 100 N (largest motor; after snapping, the chosen motor's rating) |
| C6 | hover thrust per rotor, loaded and empty | 8–40 N (oracle range, so M3 is never extrapolated) |
| C7 | snapped battery | E ≤ 2000 Wh |

**Required radius from the map:** flight distances around both no-fly zones (shortest path on a visibility graph) from the nearest candidate hub. P17 is 18.3 km away, so 18.4 km serves every PHC; 4 hubs need 19.7 km, 3 hubs 23.9 km, 2 hubs 33.4 km (no design reaches it).

**Method:**

- *MDA:* eq. (1) couples mass and motor size through T_max; fixed point m ← F(m) with slope 0.008g = 0.078, so the error falls ~13× per step; 8–10 iterations reach 1e-8 kg from 1, 10 or 40 kg
- *MDF:* MDA inside the objective, SLSQP from 8 Latin-hypercube starts. *IDF:* m as a 6th variable with m − F(x, m) = 0
- gradients by complex step, exact through the iteration

**Result (baseline C, R_req 18.4 km, FM0 0.65, plate 0.25 kg):**

- m\* = 9.59 kg: D 0.785 m, E 605 Wh, d 40 mm, t 1.46 mm, V 15.7 m/s
- active: radius (λ = 0.91), first frequency (λ = 0.33), d upper bound; stress, deflection and d/t keep large margins
- dm\*/dm_pay = 1.87 kg/kg, dm\*/dR_req = 0.49 kg/km; at the same take-off mass **each extra 100 g costs 0.38 km of radius** (re-solve 0.381); for a built drone with its battery fixed, 0.14 km

**Verification:** MDA convergence plot; code matches a line-by-line hand calculation to 1e-14; FD at h = 1e-4 and 1e-6 matches complex step (central < 1e-6, forward error ∝ h); MDF and IDF give the same mass to < 1e-9 % for all 5 designs.

**Key assumptions and data issues handled:**

- *Still air:* every design is sized with w = 0, as eq. (5) defines R; headwind is checked later (M4 average wind, M5 hourly, M7 worst week). Sizing for a 6.4 m/s headwind was rejected (+1.6 kg, no 3-hub network)
- *FM and plate:* FM0 = 0.65 and plate 0.25 kg here; M3 replaces FM (at loaded and empty hover thrust, also used in cruise power, eq. 4) and M2 the plate (0.109 kg); the final set is on the M3 card
- *Mission:* out loaded with 2.5 kg, back empty, 60 s hover at each end, straight legs at constant V; no climb or descent energy beyond the hover; the 15 % reserve and 80 % depth of discharge are the only margins
- *Snapping:* 1–4 identical packs in parallel; catalogue masses (only the 28 tubes that exist); V re-chosen on a 0.5 m/s grid; lightest feasible combination kept, all constraints re-checked
- *Optimiser:* SLSQP is local; the best of the 8 starts is taken as the answer
