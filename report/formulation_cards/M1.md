# M1 — Vehicle MDO: formulation card

_Draft written with AI help from the code in `src/m1_vehicle/`. **Rewrite in your own words before hand-in** (rule 1)._

**Question.** What is the lightest quadcopter that carries 2.5 kg to a given radius, and how does that change as the radius the network needs changes?

**Decision variables** (all continuous): rotor diameter D 0.35–0.80 m, battery energy E 200–2000 Wh, arm tube outer diameter d 15–40 mm, wall t 0.5–2.5 mm, cruise speed V 8–22 m/s. After optimising, the parts become categorical: battery pack and count, motor, tube, and prop size in whole inches.

**Objective.** Minimise take-off mass m for a required radius R_req. Why not maximise radius: that optimum sits on the D, E and d upper bounds (20.1 kg, 27.3 km), its hover thrust (49 N loaded) is outside the M3 oracle's 8–40 N range, and drone capex grows by ₹45,000 per kg. Min-mass also gives a family of designs, one per R_req, which M4 and M6 need.

**Required radius comes from the map.** I computed flight distances around both no-fly zones (shortest path on a visibility graph). P17 is 18.3 km from its nearest candidate hub, so 18.4 km lets every PHC be served. Four hubs need 19.7 km, three need 23.9 km, and two need 33.4 km, which no design reaches. R is in still air, as eq. (5) defines it. Headwind is checked later in M4/M5/M7 with the brief's model R(1 − w²/V²).

**Constraints (g ≥ 0, normalised).** R ≥ R_req. Bending stress ≤ 200 MPa. Tip deflection ≤ 0.01 L. f1 ≥ 1.15 × V_tip/(πD). d/t ≤ 40. T_max = 2mg/4 ≤ 100 N (largest motor; after snapping, the chosen motor's rating). Hover thrust per rotor 8–40 N, loaded and empty, so the M3 surrogate is never extrapolated. Snapped designs also need E ≤ 2000 Wh.

**MDA.** Eq. (1) couples mass and motor size through T_max. I iterate m ← F(m). The slope is 0.008g = 0.078, so the error falls about 13× per step, and 8–10 iterations reach 1e-8 kg from any start (1, 10 or 40 kg).

**Method.** MDF: the MDA runs inside the objective, and SLSQP runs from 8 Latin-hypercube starts. IDF: m becomes a 6th variable with the constraint m − F(x, m) = 0. Gradients are by complex step, exact through the iteration.

**Results (baseline C, R_req 18.4 km).** m* = 9.59 kg, with D = 0.785 m, E = 605 Wh, d = 40 mm, t = 1.46 mm and V = 15.7 m/s. Active constraints: radius (λ = 0.91), first frequency (λ = 0.33), and the d upper bound. Stress, deflection and d/t keep large margins. **Payload multiplier:** dm*/dm_pay = 1.87 kg/kg and dm*/dR_req = 0.49 kg/km. At the same take-off mass, **each extra 100 g costs 0.38 km of radius** (a re-solve gives 0.381). For an already-built drone with its battery fixed, the cost is 0.14 km.

**Verification** (appendix): MDA convergence plot; code matches a line-by-line hand calculation to 1e-14; FD at h = 1e-4 and 1e-6 matches complex step (central < 1e-6, forward error ∝ h); MDF and IDF give the same mass to < 1e-9 % for all 5 designs.

**Key assumptions and data issues handled.**
- *Still air:* every design is sized with w = 0, as eq. (5) defines R; headwind is checked later (M4 average wind, M5 hourly, M7 worst week). Sizing for a 6.4 m/s headwind was rejected (+1.6 kg, no 3-hub network).
- *FM:* constant FM0 = 0.65 here; M3 replaces it via `Params.fm`, evaluated at the loaded and empty hover thrust per rotor, and the same FM is used in the cruise power, eq. (4).
- *Plate:* 0.25 kg until M2 replaces it via `Params.m_plate` (re-run with 0.109 kg).
- *Mission:* out loaded with 2.5 kg, back empty, 60 s hover at each end, straight legs at constant V; no climb or descent energy beyond the hover. The 15 % reserve and 80 % depth of discharge are the only margins.
- *Required radius:* one way, from the nearest candidate hub, shortest path around both no-fly zones (corners pushed out 0.1 %).
- *Added constraints (not in the brief):* hover thrust 8–40 N loaded and empty (the oracle range, so M3 never extrapolates); T_max ≤ 100 N (largest motor).
- *Snapping:* 1–4 identical packs in parallel with E ≤ 2000 Wh; motor, tube and pack masses taken from the catalogue (only the 28 tubes that exist); props in whole inches, 14–31 in; V re-chosen on a 0.5 m/s grid; lightest feasible combination kept.
- *Optimiser:* SLSQP from 8 Latin-hypercube starts; the best local optimum is taken as the answer.
