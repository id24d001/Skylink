# M6 — Simulation-based multi-objective optimisation: formulation card

_At most one page. **AI-drafted — rewrite in your own words before submission.** Status: 🟡 draft._

**Question:** on top of each M4 network, which fleet, bays, spare packs and reserve give the best trade-off between annual cost, S45 and road share, judged by the M5 simulator and not by averages?

**Decision variables** (brief M6 list only). Vehicle design ∈ {C, D, E} (categorical). Hubs and PHC → hub assignments are fixed at the M4 solution for that design. Per open hub h: drones n_h ∈ {n_h^M4, …, n_h^M4 + 3}, spare packs s_h ∈ {0, …, 2 n_h} and bays b_h ∈ {1, …, n_h + s_h} (all integers). Emergency reserve fraction r ∈ [0, 0.5] (continuous, one value for the network). Each hub holds k_h = round-half-up(r·n_h) drones back.

**Objectives** (brief): min annual cost C (₹ cr/yr); max S45 (mean daily share of emergencies delivered ≤ 45 min); min road share (road trips ÷ all orders). P95 emergency time is reported, not optimised.

**Cost model** (M4's, from costs.yaml): hubs (capex × CRF(10 %, 10 y) + opex), drones ((2 lakh + 45 000·m) × (CRF(10 %, 3 y) + 0.15)), bays (90 000 × CRF(10 %, 5 y)), operators (Σ max(1, ⌈n_h/4⌉) × 3 shifts × 1.2 × ₹4.2 lakh), pack wear (₹22/Wh × E ÷ 500 per sortie), spare packs (₹22/Wh × E × 10 %/yr), energy (grid ₹9, genset ₹28 per kWh) and road trips (₹2 500 emergency, ₹500 routine/lab). Sorties, kWh and road trips come from the simulator.

**Constraints:** C ≤ ₹5.57 cr/yr (M0 cap; constrained domination). Every PHC is served by its M4 hub (fixed). Bounds above. All M5 rules are unchanged. **S45 ≥ 0.90 is not a constraint.** M5 showed wind caps S45 at about 0.88–0.89, so as a constraint it would leave no feasible set. 0.90 is a reference line, and the choice of point is M7's decision rule.

**Method:** NSGA-II (own code, SBX η = 5, mutation η = 10, population 32 × 40 generations) run once per design, with the fronts merged. Every candidate is simulated on the same 73 (wind day, demand seed) pairs from M5 (CRN), so candidate comparisons are paired, and results are cached by configuration. Knee = the point on the cost–S45 front farthest from the chord between its ends (both objectives scaled to [0, 1]). Weighted sum: the same GA with one objective, w·Ĉ + (1−w)·(1 − Ŝ45) for w = 0.1…0.9 on the slice, and equal weights on all three objectives for the full problem.

**Verification:** (1) the engine on ZDT1; (2) brief: weighted-sum points on or behind the NSGA-II front on the design-D cost–S45 slice; (3) front points, knee and baselines re-simulated on all 365 days with fresh seeds (out-of-sample, in place of the surrogate check); (4) fixed cost terms of "D as M4" vs M4's breakdown.

**Key assumptions and data issues handled** (the brief does not fix these):
1. Designs A and B are left out because they are infeasible in M4 (they cannot reach every PHC). Each design keeps its M4 hubs and assignments. *User-confirmed.*
2. Bounds: drones from the M4 count (keeps M4's 70 % rule) up to +3; spares ≤ 2 per drone; bays ≤ drones + spares (a bay with no pack to charge is useless); r ≤ 0.5 and one r for the network. *User-confirmed.*
3. Spare-pack cost = capital tied up, price × 10 % a year. costs.yaml gives only ₹22/Wh and 500 cycles, and wear is already charged per sortie (as in M4), so a spare adds no extra wear. *User-confirmed* (alternative: annuitise over 3 years, about 4× more and still small).
4. Annual cost = mean simulated day × 365, with M5's day draws (uniform, with replacement, from the 365 wind days).
5. Road cost counts every road trip of every class. M4 counted only emergencies in grounded hours, so M6 costs are not comparable with M4's ₹1.69 cr. "D as M4" costs ₹3.34 cr/yr on 365 days, because 46 % of its orders go by road.
6. NSGA-II rather than a 50-run surrogate (brief's option 1), because one candidate takes about 0.3 s per 73 days on 4 cores. *User-confirmed.*
7. Noise: with CRN each candidate's objectives are deterministic, so the risk is over-fitting the 73 days, which is why the 365-day re-simulation is done. Final numbers are quoted on 365 days.
8. Out of scope (not M6 decisions in the brief): headwind range margin in M1, an extra hub, re-assignment of PHCs, drone availability < 100 % and demand growth (M7).

**Result** (365 days): the front has 178 points at **₹2.12–2.94 cr/yr**. All of them cost less than the M4-sized networks (₹3.3–3.5 cr), because spare packs and bays cut road share from 45 % to 6–9 %. **Knee: design E**, with drones 4/3/4 at H1/H2/H4, spares 4/4/6, bays 3/2/4 and one reserve drone per hub. It costs **₹2.25 cr/yr**, with **S45 0.889 ± 0.015** and road share 10 % (0.901 and 8.7 % on the 73 search days). The equal-weight weighted sum picks a neighbour of the knee (E, ₹2.29 cr, S45 0.889). No configuration reaches 0.90 on 365 days; the best is 0.889. Holding one reserve drone per hub buys about 1 pp of S45 at the cost of about 2 pp more road share. P95 stays at 150 min everywhere, because 11–13 % of emergencies still go by road (gusts plus headwind range).
