# M6 — Simulation-based multi-objective optimisation: formulation card

**Question.** On top of each M4 network, which fleet, bays, spare packs and reserve give the best trade-off between annual cost, S45 and road share, judged by the M5 simulator and not by averages?

**Decision variables:**

- vehicle design ∈ {C, D, E}, **categorical**; hubs and PHC → hub assignment fixed at that design's M4 solution
- per open hub h, **integer**: drones n_h ∈ {n_h^M4, …, n_h^M4 + 3}; spare packs s_h ∈ {0, …, 2n_h}; bays b_h ∈ {1, …, n_h + s_h}
- emergency reserve fraction r ∈ [0, 0.5], **continuous**, one value for the network; each hub holds k_h = round-half-up(r·n_h) drones back

**Objectives:**

- O1 min annual cost C (₹ crore/yr)
- O2 max S45 (mean daily share of emergencies delivered ≤ 45 min)
- O3 min road share (road trips ÷ all orders). P95 emergency time is reported only.

**Constraints:**

| # | Constraint | Number |
|----|--------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| C1 | annual cost (M0 C7) | ≤ ₹5.57 crore/yr (constrained domination) |
| C2 | every PHC served | by its M4 hub (fixed) |
| C3 | variable bounds | as above |
| C4 | operating rules | every M5 rule unchanged |

S45 ≥ 0.90 is **not** a constraint: M5 showed wind caps S45 at about 0.88–0.89, so it would leave no feasible set. 0.90 is a reference line, and the choice of point is M7's decision rule.

**Cost model (M4's, from costs.yaml):** hubs (capex × CRF(10 %, 10 y) + opex); drones ((₹2 lakh + ₹45,000·m) × (CRF(10 %, 3 y) + 0.15)); bays (₹90,000 × CRF(10 %, 5 y)); operators (Σ max(1, ⌈n_h/4⌉) × 3 shifts × 1.2 × ₹4.2 lakh); pack wear (₹22/Wh × E ÷ 500 per sortie); spare packs (₹22/Wh × E × 10 %/yr); energy (₹9 grid, ₹28 genset per kWh); road (₹2,500 emergency, ₹500 routine/lab). Sorties, kWh and road trips come from the simulator.

**Method:**

- NSGA-II (own code; SBX η = 5, mutation η = 10; population 32 × 40 generations), run once per design, fronts merged
- every candidate simulated on the same 73 (wind day, demand seed) pairs from M5 (CRN), so comparisons are paired; results cached by configuration
- *Knee:* the point on the cost–S45 front farthest from the chord between its ends (both scaled to [0, 1])
- *Weighted sum:* same GA, one objective w·Ĉ + (1 − w)·(1 − Ŝ45), w = 0.1…0.9 on the design-D slice; equal weights on all three objectives for the full problem

**Result:**

- front: 178 points at ₹2.12–2.94 crore/yr (73 search days), all cheaper than the M4-sized networks (₹3.3–3.5 crore on 365 days), because spare packs and bays cut road share from 45 % to 6–9 %
- **Knee: design E** (hubs H1, H2, H4), drones 4/3/4, spares 4/4/6, bays 3/2/4, one reserve drone per hub: **₹2.25 crore/yr, S45 0.889 ± 0.015**, road share 10 % on 365 days (0.901 and 8.7 % on the 73 search days)
- equal-weight weighted sum picks a neighbour of the knee (E, ₹2.29 crore, S45 0.889)
- no configuration reaches 0.90 on 365 days (best 0.889); one reserve drone per hub buys ~1 pp of S45 for ~2 pp more road share; P95 stays 150 min, as 11–13 % of emergencies still go by road (gusts plus headwind range)

**Verification:** (1) the NSGA-II engine on ZDT1; (2) weighted-sum points on or behind the NSGA-II front on the design-D cost–S45 slice; (3) front points, knee and baselines re-simulated on all 365 days with fresh seeds (out-of-sample check); (4) fixed cost terms of "D as M4" vs M4's breakdown.

**Key assumptions and data issues handled:**

- *Designs:* A and B left out (infeasible in M4, cannot reach every PHC); each design keeps its M4 hubs and assignments
- *Bounds:* drones from the M4 count (keeps M4's 70 % rule) up to +3; spares ≤ 2 per drone; bays ≤ drones + spares (a bay with no pack to charge is useless); r ≤ 0.5, one r for the network
- *Spare-pack cost:* capital tied up, price × 10 %/yr; wear is already charged per sortie (as in M4), so a spare adds no extra wear (annuitising over 3 years would be ~4× more and still small)
- *Annual cost:* mean simulated day × 365. Road cost counts every road trip of every class, so M6 costs are not comparable with M4's ₹1.69 crore: "D as M4" costs ₹3.34 crore/yr on 365 days, as 46 % of its orders go by road
- *NSGA-II rather than a 50-run surrogate:* one candidate takes ~0.3 s per 73 days on 4 cores
- *Noise:* with CRN each candidate's objectives are deterministic, so the risk is over-fitting the 73 days; hence the 365-day re-simulation, and final numbers are quoted on 365 days
- *Out of scope:* headwind range margin in M1, an extra hub, re-assigning PHCs, drone availability < 100 % and demand growth (M7)
