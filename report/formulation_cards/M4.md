# M4 — Hub network and fleet: formulation card

_At most one page. **AI-drafted — rewrite in your own words before submission.** Status: 🟡 draft._

**Question this module answers:** for each M1 vehicle (A–E), which candidate sites should become hubs, which hub serves each PHC (or does it go by road), and how many drones, charging bays and operators does each hub need, at the lowest annual cost?

**Decision variables** (one MILP per design, 8 hubs × 24 PHCs, 248 variables):
- y_j ∈ {0,1}: open candidate site j (H0–H6, P09).
- x_ij ∈ {0,1}: PHC i flown from hub j. r_i ∈ {0,1}: PHC i served by road.
- n_j, b_j, o_j ∈ ℤ≥0: drones, charging bays, and operators per shift at hub j.
- The vehicle design is categorical. It is fixed inside each MILP and varied in an outer loop.

**Objective:** minimise annual cost (₹/yr). Capital is annualised with CRF at 10 %.
- Hub ₹6.93 lakh. Drone (₹2 lakh + ₹45k/kg MTOW) × (CRF 3 y + 15 % maintenance). Bay ₹23.7k. Operator ₹15.1 lakh per shift position (3 shifts × 1.2 relief).
- Per PHC served by drone: energy (₹9/kWh grid; ₹28/kWh genset in the Mon/Wed/Fri 14–17 h outage), battery wear (₹22/Wh ÷ 500 cycles), and road trips for the orders that fall in gust-grounded hours.
- Per PHC served by road: every order at ₹2,500 (emergency) or ₹500 (routine, lab).

**Constraints:**
1. Each PHC is served once: Σ_j x_ij + r_i = 1.
2. Only open hubs serve: x_ij ≤ y_j. This is the tight form, so no big-M is needed here.
3. Reach: x_ij = 0 unless the no-fly path satisfies L_ij ≤ R(1 − w²/V²), with w = 6.4 m/s (P90 of flyable hours). R already holds the 15 % energy reserve.
4. Drone time, for each of the 24 hours: Σ_i x_ij · (orders in hour h) · (2L_ijV/(V²−w²) + 10 min) ≤ 0.70 × 60 × 0.945 × n_j. Here 0.945 is the flyable share of hours. The peak hour binds.
5. Bay energy, for each hour: recharge Wh (trip energy ÷ 0.9) ≤ 0.70 × 1.5 kW × b_j. Also b_j ≥ y_j.
6. Staff: 4·o_j ≥ n_j, o_j ≥ y_j, n_j ≥ y_j.
7. Big-M: n_j ≤ N_max,j·y_j (likewise for b, o). N_max,j is the fleet hub j would need if it flew every PHC it can reach, so the bound never cuts off a feasible plan.
8. Emergency guard: PHCs sent by road carry ≤ 5 % of daily emergencies. This keeps the M0 floor of S45 ≥ 90 % within reach.

**Inputs / outputs:**
- In: M1/M3 surrogate-FM designs (`m1_fm_designs.csv`: m, R, V, powers), flight distances around the no-fly zones (`m1_flight_distances.csv`), demand, hourly profile (rescaled), wind, and costs.
- Out: `ctx["m4_network"]`, holding hubs, assignments, n, b and o per design, for M5 (simulate) and M6 (tune n, b, spares and reserve on top).

**Method and why:** `scipy.optimize.milp` (HiGHS branch-and-cut).
- It solves to proven optimality (gap 0) in under 1 s per design.
- It needs no new dependency, and the instance is small.
- Hourly capacity rows replace one daily average, so the 10–11 h peak sizes the fleet.

**Result (6.4 m/s):** all three feasible designs are below the ₹5.57 crore all-road cap.

| Design | Annual cost | Status |
|---|---|---|
| A | ₹4.96 cr | infeasible with the guard (72 % of emergencies by road) |
| B | ₹3.27 cr | infeasible with the guard (22 % of emergencies by road) |
| C | ₹2.84 cr | feasible, P17 by road |
| D | ₹2.65 cr | feasible, P17 by road |
| E | **₹2.57 cr** | feasible: 5 hubs (H1 H2 H4 H5 P09), 17 drones, 13 bays, no road PHCs |

Design E is the only design that flies every PHC at this wind.

**Verification:**
- Brute force over all reachable assignments on 4 hubs × 8 PHCs matches the MILP in 10 of 10 cases (5 designs × guard on/off) to 1e-16, with the same assignment.
- LP-relaxation gap is 4–8 %. It comes from rounding up n, b and o, not from y.
- Doubling the big-M leaves the optimum unchanged.
- Cost coefficients match a hand calculation.

**Key assumptions:**
- Mean Poisson rates per hour, with queueing left to M5.
- Lab trips are counted as out-and-back sorties, like deliveries.
- The 2 min of hover is inside the 10 min ground time.
- A PHC that is itself the hub (P09) needs no flight.
- In an outage only 2 bays can charge (3 kW genset). That is left to M5/M6 spare batteries, not modelled here.
