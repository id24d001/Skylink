# M4 — Hub network and fleet: formulation card

_At most one page. **AI-drafted — rewrite in your own words before submission.** Status: 🟡 draft._

**Question this module answers:** for each M1 vehicle (A–E), which candidate sites become hubs, which hub serves each PHC, and how many drones and charging bays each hub needs, at the lowest annual cost. Solving once per design gives annual cost against vehicle choice.

**Decision variables** (one MILP per design; 8 hubs × 24 PHCs):
- y_j ∈ {0,1}: open site j (H0–H6, P09).
- x_ij ∈ {0,1}: PHC i served by hub j.
- n_j ∈ ℤ≥0: drones at hub j. b_j ∈ ℤ≥0: charging bays at hub j. These four are the brief's variables.
- o_j ∈ ℤ≥0: operators per shift. This is an auxiliary variable that makes "1 per 4 drones, at least 1 per open hub" linear.
- The vehicle design is categorical, fixed in each MILP and looped outside.

**Objective:** minimise annual cost (₹/yr), using only the brief's cost list and costs.yaml (CRF at 10 %):
- Hubs: ₹6.93 lakh each.
- Drones: (₹2 lakh + ₹45k/kg MTOW) × (CRF 3 y + 15 %). Battery packs are added at one 500-cycle pack-life per sortie, since every sortie ends in a swap.
- Bays: ₹23.7k each.
- Operators: ₹15.1 lakh per post (3 shifts × 1.2 relief).
- Energy: ₹9/kWh grid; ₹28/kWh genset in the Mon/Wed/Fri 14–17 h outage.
- Road fallback: emergencies placed in a gust-grounded hour wait over 30 min, so they go by road at ₹2,500 each.

**Constraints:**
1. Every PHC is served by one hub: Σ_j x_ij = 1. The brief promises delivery to every PHC, and M0 made 24/24 coverage hard.
2. Only open hubs serve: x_ij ≤ y_j. This tight form needs no big-M.
3. Reach: x_ij = 0 unless the no-fly path satisfies L_ij ≤ R, the still-air M1 radius from eq. 5, which already includes the 15 % reserve ("within your M1 range with reserve"). Emergency delivery time 3 + L/V + 1 + 1 ≤ 45 min is also checked; it never binds (longest is 24.9 min).
4. Drone time: Σ_i x_ij · (sorties/day) · (flight + 2 min hover + 10 min ground) ≤ 0.70 × 1440 × 0.945 × n_j. The brief caps utilisation at 70 %. The 0.945 is the share of hours a drone may take off (gust < 14 m/s). The sums use mean daily demand ("the MILP uses averages", brief M5). Flight time uses the brief's headwind model, averaged over flyable hours (×1.05–1.10).
5. Bays must recharge each day's sorties: bay-hours ≤ 24·b_j, and ≤ (24 − 1.29)·b_j + 2 × 1.29, because a 3 kW genset runs only 2 bays during the 1.29 h/day average outage.
6. 4·o_j ≥ n_j; o_j ≥ y_j; n_j ≥ y_j; b_j ≥ y_j.
7. Big-M: n_j ≤ N_max·y_j (the same for b and o). N_max is what hub j would need if it served every PHC it reaches, so the bound never cuts off a feasible plan.

**Inputs / outputs:**
- In: M1 designs re-run with the M3 surrogate FM (`m1_fm_designs.csv`: m, R, V, E, powers); M1 flight distances around the no-fly zones; demand; hourly profile (rescaled; used for the grounded-emergency share); wind; costs.
- Out: `ctx["m4_network"]` (hubs, assignments, n, b, o per design), for M5 to simulate and M6 to tune drones, bays, spares and reserve.

**Method and why:** `scipy.optimize.milp` (HiGHS branch-and-cut). It gives proven optimality in under 1 s per design, needs no new dependency, and the instance is small.

**Result:**

| Design | Annual cost | Network |
|---|---|---|
| A | ₹3.73 cr (what-if) | infeasible: 6 PHCs out of reach |
| B | ₹2.21 cr (what-if) | infeasible: P17 out of reach |
| C | ₹1.83 cr | 5 hubs |
| **D** | **₹1.69 cr** | 4 hubs (H1 H2 H6 P09), 8 drones, 4 bays |
| E | ₹1.76 cr | 3 hubs |

The A and B what-ifs send only their unreachable PHCs by road, to price full coverage. Every feasible design is far below the ₹5.57 cr all-road cap.

**Verification:**
- Brute force on 4 hubs × 8 PHCs equals the MILP in 10/10 cases, with the same cost and assignment.
- The LP-relaxation gap is 2.4–4.7 %. The LP already opens whole hubs; the gap comes from rounding up n, b and o.
- Doubling the big-M changes nothing.
- The hand check matches exactly.

**Key assumptions:**
- Averages only. Queues, waits over 30 min and the peak hour are left to M5; the 8 drones are a floor that M5/M6 will raise.
- Routine ("same day") and lab orders placed in a grounded hour wait and are still flown.
- Lab pick-ups are the same out-and-back sortie.
- P09 served from a hub at P09 needs no sortie.
